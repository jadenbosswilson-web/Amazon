The Meal Prepper's Guide to Not Wasting Food

Most food waste isn't a cooking problem — it's a storage problem. A few container upgrades fix more of it than you'd expect.

The real reason food gets wasted

It's rarely that people cook too much. It's that leftovers go into containers that don't seal well, get pushed to the back of the fridge, and are forgotten until they're no longer good. Visibility and a proper seal solve most of this.

Airtight, stackable containers

A matched set that stacks cleanly does two things at once: it keeps food fresher for longer by actually sealing out air, and it makes the fridge easier to scan at a glance, so nothing gets lost behind a jar of condiments.

A simple system

Label with a date, store the oldest items in front, and portion things into single-meal containers rather than one big batch — you're far more likely to actually eat a single-serving container than to commit to finishing a giant one. None of this requires a complicated system, just containers that make it easy to see what you have.

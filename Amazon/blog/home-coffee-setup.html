Building a Home Coffee & Latte Setup Without a Barista Budget

You don't need a $2,000 espresso machine to stop overpaying for coffee out. Here's a setup that covers most of what people actually order.

Start with what you actually drink

Before buying anything, it's worth being honest about your order. If it's black coffee, you need almost nothing. If it's lattes and foamed milk drinks, the frother matters more than the machine.

The base: a reliable single-serve machine

A pod-based machine removes the two biggest barriers to making coffee at home consistently: cleanup and timing. It's not going to replace a café espresso shot, but it removes the excuse of 'I don't have time' on a weekday morning.

Small Kitchen, Big Upgrade: Space-Saving Tools That Punch Above Their Size

The real constraint in a small kitchen

It's rarely square footage — it's clutter from tools that only do one job. The fix is picking a smaller number of tools that each do more, rather than a full drawer of single-purpose gadgets.

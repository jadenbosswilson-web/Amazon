5 Kitchen Gadgets Actually Worth Buying in 2026

Most single-use gadgets end up in a drawer within a month. These didn't — here's the actual test we use before recommending anything.

The test: would you buy it again?

Kitchen gadgets have a bad reputation, and mostly it's deserved. For every tool that earns a permanent spot on the counter, there are a dozen that get used twice and then live in a drawer forever. Before anything makes it onto this list, we ask one question: is this something people keep using a year later, not just the week they bought it?

That rules out most novelty items and leaves a short list of genuinely useful tools — the kind that save real time on tasks you already do every day, not tasks you invented to justify the purchase.

1. A chopper that replaces three knives

A good multi-blade chopper doesn't replace knife skills, it replaces the fifteen minutes of repetitive dicing that most people avoid cooking to skip. Look for one with interchangeable blades and a container that goes straight from prep to storage.
